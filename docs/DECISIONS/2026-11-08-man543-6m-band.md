# MAN-543: 6m is a named band, and "all" adds it only when the tuning range reaches it

Date: 2026-11-08
Status: implemented.

`[input] bands = ["6m"]` was refused as an unknown band name, because
`manta_server::band_plan::CW_SEGMENTS` stopped at 10 m. Everything after the
table already handled 50 MHz: `band.rs` labels 6 m spots, and a hand-computed
`freq_hz = 50050000.0` or `bands = ["50050"]` planned and spotted correctly.
RBN carries 6 m CW spots, so an operator with a 50 MHz-capable receiver
should be able to name the band.

This record supersedes the "6 m" part of the "6 m and up" item in the
"Not yet" list of the [MAN-310 band-plan record](2026-10-17-man310-band-plan.md).
The rest of that item (2 m and up) stays not yet.

## Decisions

### D1: one row, 50.000 to 50.100 MHz, no beacon

`CW_SEGMENTS` gains `seg("6m", 50000.0, 50100.0, None)` after 10 m. The span
is the CW-only part of the 6 m band plans. Its bottom is the 6 m beacon
sub-band, so beacons there are decoded and spotted as ordinary CW.
`beacon_hz` stays `None`: the column is the NCDXF/IARU beacon that
`calibrate` also uses (`band_streams.rs` holds the two equal), and NCDXF has
no 6 m beacon.

### D2: "all" is HF, plus 6m when the tuning range overlaps it

`"all"` expands to every HF segment (span top at or below
`band_plan::HF_TOP_HZ`, 30 MHz, `CwSegment::is_hf`) plus any other segment
whose span overlaps `tuning_range_hz` by more than zero width (the same test
`plan()` uses to skip a band). This is the ticket's second scenario: `"all"`
still means the HF set unless the receiver's range includes 50 MHz. An
ungated row would add a `6m skipped` note to every default `"all"` plan.
`DEFAULT_TUNING_RANGE_HZ` is now written as `(0.0, HF_TOP_HZ)`; its value is
unchanged.

### D2a: `["all", "6m"]` follows membership

A named band listed with `"all"` is refused only when it is in the `"all"`
set. On a range that reaches 50 MHz, `["all", "6m"]` is refused with the
existing "which \"all\" already includes" sentence. On the default range it
plans the 14 HF streams plus one note, `6m skipped: 50000 to 50100 kHz is
outside the receiver's tuning range, 0 to 30000 kHz`.

### D3: `tuning_range_hz` keeps its 0 to 30 MHz default

A 50 MHz default would give every KiwiSDR and HF-only SDR an unreachable
band. The operator declares the range, as the `["50050"]` workaround already
required. `bands = ["6m"]` on the default range is the existing "leaves
nothing to skim" error.

### D4: the beacon sub-band is noted in the docs, not printed at run time

`StreamPlan::notes()` is the skip and clip sentences by contract (MAN-310),
and no other band's segment is described at run time. `config check` already
lists the centre as `freq_hz=50050000`. Sources disagree on the exact beacon
edges by region and year, so docs/CONFIG.md says "the bottom of the segment"
and gives no kHz edges.

### D5: width is documented, not changed

100 kHz needs a stream of at least 110.5 kHz to fit one stream
(100 / 0.9052). At 96 kS/s 6 m is two streams, and hpsdr and soapy refuse
that with their existing message. At 192 kS/s it is one stream at
50050 kHz.

## Consequence

An `"all"` entry whose declared range already reached 50 MHz now also plans
6 m. That can push a plan that was accepted before over the one-stream limit
of hpsdr and soapy, or over the 16-input cap (for example soapy at
192 kS/s with `tuning_range_hz = [28100000.0, 60000000.0]`). Every plan on a
range below 50 MHz, including the default, is unchanged. `bands` applies
only to live inputs, so file-input decoder output does not change.
